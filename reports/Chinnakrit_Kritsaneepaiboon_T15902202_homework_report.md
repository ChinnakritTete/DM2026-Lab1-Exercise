I loaded `newdataset/Reddit-stock-sentiment.csv` using `load_dataset_tool` (`load_dataset_1`), with `text` as the document column and `label` as the label column. The dataset contained **847 posts**: 423 neutral, 315 negative, and 109 positive. Data inspection (`inspect_data_2`) found no missing texts but identified 23 duplicate posts, which I kept. Document-length statistics (`describe_data_5`) showed a right-skewed distribution, with a mean of 148.68 characters and a median of 79.

The default DTM build (`dtm_6`) failed because of incorrect parameter types. Setting `min_df=0` and `max_df=1.0` produced 4,346 terms with 99.5329% sparsity. Term frequencies (`tf_7`) were dominated by stop words, while the heatmap (`dtm_heatmap_8`) showed mostly zeros. Variance filtering (`var_filter_9`) retained 506 terms. Pearson and Spearman filtering (`pearson_filter_10`, `spearman_filter_11`) ranked potentially relevant words, but zero thresholds retained all terms. The feature correlation matrix (`feature_corr_12`) also showed strong correlations between common words.

For pattern mining, FP-Growth with `min_sup=5` found 592 neutral patterns (`mine_patterns_13`) and 57 negative patterns (`mine_patterns_14`). Neutral patterns included repeated cryptocurrency terms, while negative patterns contained words such as `delusional` and `worst`. Positive-class mining failed due to a `UnicodeDecodeError`. PCA (`reduce_dimensions_15`) explained 27.0% and 4.99% of variance in PC1 and PC2, respectively. PCA, t-SNE, and UMAP showed substantial overlap between sentiment classes. Label binarization (`binarize_labels_18`) produced a `[847, 3]` matrix, while cosine similarity (`cosine_sim_19`) between documents 0 and 1 was 0.0.

Overall, the pipeline revealed limitations caused by incorrect DTM parameters, encoding issues, duplicate posts, and the influence of stop words. To improve the analysis, I would correct parameter types, standardize temporary files to UTF-8, remove duplicates and stop words, apply non-zero correlation thresholds, and use TF-IDF features to better identify sentiment-related patterns.
